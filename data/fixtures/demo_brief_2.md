Project brief — FreshRoute (grocery delivery operator)

We want a dispatch platform similar to what you built for other logistics clients.

- Back-office admin to manage orders, couriers and vans.
- Dispatcher dashboard with live map of courier positions.
- Integration with our ERP (Microsoft Dynamics) to import orders and sync delivery status.
- Maps integration for routing and ETAs.
- SMS and email notifications to customers.
- Courier mobile app screens with proof of delivery photos.
- KPI reporting dashboard and Excel export.
- Import historical orders from spreadsheets.
- GDPR compliance for courier location data.
- Maybe single sign-on with Google Workspace later?
